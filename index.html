01 / THE MATHEMATICS

A little time.
A big difference.

A common year has 365 days. Earth needs about 365.2422. That tiny decimal adds up.

EARTH’S APPROXIMATE YEAR
365.2422
365calendar days
+
0.2422extra day each year

That’s about 5 hours, 49 minutes of extra time.

0.2422 × 4 = 0.9688 days

After four years, we’re almost a whole day behind. So we add February 29.
